# Public Disclosure Boundary

This showcase exists to demonstrate the product direction and maturity of Saad Marketing OS without exposing production intellectual property or customer information.

## Safe to publish

- product problem and target user;
- high-level workflow and product principles;
- generic capability descriptions;
- synthetic interface examples;
- broad technology categories;
- current product stage and non-confidential roadmap themes.

## Keep private

- system prompts and prompt templates;
- agent definitions, orchestration logic and internal evaluation methods;
- scoring formulas, decision rules and proprietary quality gates;
- production source code, schemas, migrations and access-control policies;
- infrastructure identifiers, private endpoints, credentials and environment values;
- customer or pilot names, briefs, documents, metrics, screenshots and results;
- confidential pricing experiments, contracts and investment discussions.

## Reviewer access

Qualified reviewers may receive limited private technical access when necessary. Such access should be scoped, time-bounded and provided without making the production repository public.

## Publication rule

When unsure whether an item reveals implementation detail, customer information or security-sensitive context, do not publish it here.
